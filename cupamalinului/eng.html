CUPA MALINULUI

Eng/Ro

What? CUPA MALINULUI is a traditional freeride contest. The first contest took place in 1998.

Where? In Bucegi Mountain, on Malin Valley, where else:).  The starting point is 1,5 h from Babele cablecar (GPS: 45.4289°N 25.4835°E) at 2490 m altitude. The finish is 1000 m lower. The prize giving ceremony is taking place in the afternoon at Gura Diham chalet (GPS: 45.449°N 25.5201°E) or other announced location.

Who? Anyone with knowledge, guts, a pair of ski or a snowboard.

Other Q? Please contact us.

Who's behind: our Partners, TeamXpert & Asociatia TabereCuSuflet

 

 

 

CUPA MALINULUI este prima si cea mai longeviva competitie de schi & snowboard Freeride din Romania. Competitia s-a desfasurat fara intrerupere din 1998 pana in 2014.
