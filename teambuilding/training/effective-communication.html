Professional Communication

At the end of this training, the participant will learn about the Effective Communication and will efficiently transmit the work information, in order to avoid the deficient execution of a project or wrong decisions taking due to the insufficient information or the corruption of the message.

This is done by structuring the information based on the required action and verifying the understanding of the message at the destination. The structuring of the argumentation can be used in the case of public presentations, meetings or informing emails.

Training agenda:

  • Module 1: Transforming information into usable message
  • Module 2: Efficient use of language
  • Module 3: Structuring the argumentation
  • Module 4: Identifying and eliminating the causes of wrong message transmission

Course duration:

4 half day modules

Target:

Managers, PR people, sales and marketing, other people whose activity includes the transmission of information between various organizational levels.

The training includes:

  • Assessing the client's training needs
  • Feedback
  • Analysis of students’ implementation of skills 1 month and 6 months after the delivery of the training
  • Online coaching with students (by email) in the first 30 days after the training delivery.

Our trainers are certified by International Swiss Centre for Business Research, Geneva. The trainings are provided by International Swiss Centre for Business Research, Geneva. The participants receive Diploma upon graduation.
